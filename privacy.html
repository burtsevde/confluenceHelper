Privacy Policy for Confluence Helper

Confluence Helper processes information locally in your browser when you use its features.

Page access

The extension examines selected text, text colors, and page elements in the active tab to find matching text, highlight results, and activate expand controls. It does not save selected text or page content.

Local storage

The extension saves your last selected color in its local browser storage so it can restore that color when you open it again. No other page content is stored by the extension.

Sharing

The extension does not send your data to the developer or third parties. Its code makes no network requests.
